## What this screen is for

**Service Order** is where you record and review work in **Administration**. This guide shows the screen and what to tap or click, step by step.

## Before you start

- You are signed in to Medora.
- Your hospital is selected in the top bar.

## Walkthrough

### Step 1: Open the screen

Sign in with your hospital account.

Select **Testing Hospital 1** in the hospital selector if prompted.

In the left **module bar**, click the **Administration** module icon.

On the Administration home screen, open **Service Order**.

Under **Service Order**, select the **Category Master** tab (first sub-page opens by default).

_This guide shows the **Category Master** work area inside **Service Order**._

## If something goes wrong

- **Blank screen:** ask your hospital administrator to check that your account can see this screen, then reload the page.
- **Cannot save:** check required fields marked with a red star.
